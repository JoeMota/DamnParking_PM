Sample database

Fabricated lot records and occupancy history for review. These rows stand in for the two internal data groups in the Sprint 2 estimate. They are not a live camera feed and they are not an official parking inventory.

What is stored

The Live Parking Monitoring slice stores lot information and timestamped occupancy history. This page reads data/demo-db.json, exported from data/damn-parking.sqlite. Every occupancy row is marked is_sample = 1 and sourced from sample_camera_feed.

Tables

SQLite tables in damn-parking.sqlite.
Table Role Columns
lots Lot information lot_id, name, kind, address, latitude, longitude, stall_capacity, permit_type, rate_note, walk_minutes
cameras Sample camera coverage per lot camera_id, lot_id, label, covers, status
occupancy_observations Timestamped occupancy history observation_id, lot_id, observed_at, occupied_stalls, open_stalls, source, confidence, is_sample
dataset_meta Dataset label and window key, value
latest_occupancy View of the newest row per lot lot_id, name, kind, stall_capacity, observed_at, occupied_stalls, open_stalls, confidence, source
