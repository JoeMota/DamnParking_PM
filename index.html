What we’re building

Drivers circle lots while empty stalls sit out of sight. Facilities staff lack a trustworthy occupancy picture. Damn Parking proposes a computer-vision layer on existing camera feeds: classify bay occupancy, publish a live map and counts, and keep humans in the loop for exceptions — without requiring new sensors in every stall.

The 3D lot above is a cinematic stand-in for that signal — cars arriving and leaving, camera frustums tracking bays — while the sprint pages document the management work behind the idea.
