What the demo shows

One driver pulls up to Sun Bowl Garage, checks Damn Parking on his phone, parks in stall A4, and the lot camera records that stall as taken. The open count falls from 18 to 17. The picture is drawn in the browser. This is a portal demonstration, not a released app and not a live camera feed.

  1. Drive up. The view is from the driver's seat as the car enters the aisle.
  2. Phone. The phone names stall A4 and shows 18 stalls still open.
  3. Park. The car pulls into the open stall.
  4. Filled. The camera lifts, the stall marker turns taken, and the phone count drops to 17.

Pause and Replay sit under the picture. Space or K pauses. R starts over. Drive up, Phone, Park, and Filled jump to that chapter. The picture stays on the filled stall when the browser asks for reduced motion.
