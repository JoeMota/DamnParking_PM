Possible UI

Three concept screens for the driver experience. They show directions the interface could take. They are not a released app, and they do not replace the quality-plan rules for stale data.

Find, review, and reserve

A phone flow for choosing a lot, reading the stall count, and keeping a reservation. Sun Bowl Garage is the sample lot on these screens.

Map with open stalls

A dark campus map with a pin for each open-stall count and a card for the selected garage. Directions sit on the card instead of a separate page.

Search, permit, directions, and account

A second phone set that starts at search, sells a student permit, gives walking directions, and ends on the account page.

Possible UI wireframe model

Phone screens for login, sign up, available spots, and telling other drivers a stall just opened, built as a 3D wireframe. Drag to orbit, Shift-drag to pan, and scroll or pinch to zoom toward the pointer. Double-click a phone to frame it, or use Fit all for the whole flow. Keys: + or = zoom in, - zooms out, and 0 or R resets. This is a possible UI wireframe model, not a released app.
