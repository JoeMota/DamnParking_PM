Estimation appendix summary

The team estimated the Live Parking Monitoring slice (six backlog stories, 32 story points). Scope includes nearby lot views, live availability, filtering, directions, and a manager dashboard for patterns and misuse review. Out of scope: camera installation, training a new computer-vision model, payments, reservations, and automated enforcement.

Methods used: function points and story points to cost, with a four-person team at 20 project hours per person per week and a 40-hour integration and coordination allowance.

  • Range: 340 to 680 team hours
  • Expected hours (PERT): 538 hours
  • Classroom loaded rate: $60 per person-hour (assumption for this estimate only)
  • Cost range: $20,400 to $40,800; expected about $32,280
  • Calendar: about 4.25 to 8.5 weeks at 80 team hours per week; expected about 6.7 weeks
  • Appendix date: October 5, 2026 (consolidates the September 29 estimate)
